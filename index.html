A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Clear from the start.

Is FlowDesk a real business?

No. FlowDesk is a fictional service created for a website assignment. The services and workflows are illustrative, not commercial offers or claims of measured results.

Does the demo send or collect anything?

The demo only changes what you see on this page. It sends no messages and asks for no personal information. This site's own code stores only your language choice in your browser, when storage is available, and uses no analytics. Hosting providers and external sites have their own practices.

Why are there three languages?

English is the main language. Arabic and German make the project multilingual; Arabic also uses a right-to-left layout. Language switching and the interactive simulation require JavaScript.
